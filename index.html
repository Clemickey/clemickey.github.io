Product analytics

Matching KPIs to the question

In the Legends of Aether product management simulation, the work was not simply about finding numbers. It was about understanding what the team wanted to know and choosing the KPI that most directly answered the question.

  • Retention Rate for whether new players return after a period.
  • DAU for the size of the daily active player community.
  • Churn Rate for player loss over a defined period.
  • Conversion Rate for players completing a purchase.
  • Created a KPI recommendation brief and stakeholder presentation project plan.
