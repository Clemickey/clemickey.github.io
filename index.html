How I got here

I studied economics and finance because I wanted to understand how value moves, through markets, through businesses, through decisions. But a degree only gets you the theory. So while I was building that foundation, I was also out finding ways to apply it: customer facing roles, records and data work, and eventually building my own venture from scratch.

Vibeora Beauty and Wellness Co. started as a hustle. It became a real education in B2B sales, CRM systems, and what it actually takes to get a client from first contact to signed. Alongside that, an 18 month remote engagement supporting a UK based executive taught me what "reliable" means when your manager is eight time zones away and has to trust you completely.

That combination, the analytical training and the operational grind, is what I bring to a role. I don't just execute a task list. I understand why the task matters.
